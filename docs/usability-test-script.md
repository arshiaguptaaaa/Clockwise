# Clockwise usability test (15 minutes, one traveller at a time)

Goal: see whether a traveller understands a clash card and can get a fix agreed without help.
Tell them: "I'm testing the product, not you. Say what you're thinking out loud."

## Setup
- Use a throwaway trip (not the demo trip you present from). Two testers play Arshia (organiser) and Ridhima.
- Ridhima's flight is moved from 6:15 PM to 8:15 PM, so the Birthday Dinner clashes.
- Screen-record with the tester's consent. Do not record anything with real personal details.

## Tasks (do not explain the UI)
1. "You've just been told your flight is delayed. Tell the group." (Do they use the chat? Do they notice Clockwise reacting?)
2. "Look at the card Clockwise posted. What does it say happened?" (Do they read 'What changed 6:15 PM → 8:15 PM DELAYED'? Do they understand 'Lands at ≠ available at'?)
3. "Get the dinner moved." (Do they find the main button? Do they understand it only proposes, and that people vote?)
4. "Vote on it as the other traveller." (Is Accept / Decline findable? Do they notice conditions like 'yes, if it's veg'?)
5. "As the organiser, make it official." (Do they find 'Update plan'? Do they understand nothing changes before this?)
6. "Install Clockwise on your phone." (Trip menu → Install & feedback. Android: does the install prompt appear? iPhone: do they follow the Share → Add to Home Screen hint?)

## What to write down per task
- Time to finish, number of wrong taps, anything they said that shows confusion.
- Quotes, verbatim.
- Severity: blocked / slowed / minor.

## After
- Ask for the 1–5 rating in the app (Trip menu → Install & feedback) and one thing they'd change.
- Read the results next to `/admin/insights` (funnel and errors) so qualitative and quantitative evidence sit together.

## Honest limits
- 5 testers find most obvious problems; they do not give statistics. Do not report percentages from a handful of sessions.
